# Player Guide

## Welcome to INK FIGHT

INK FIGHT: Doodle Combat is a landscape mobile fighting game built around quick matches, expressive ink fighters, and readable skill-based combat.

## Starting a match

1. Open the game and wait for the ink logo to finish loading.
2. Tap **Bot Fight** from the main menu.
3. Choose a fighter and difficulty when those options are available.
4. Read the short move hint, then tap **Ready**.
5. Use the arena controls to move, attack, block, and jump.

## Winning a match

Reduce the opponent's health to zero before the timer ends. If the timer expires, the fighter with more remaining health wins. A tie is recorded as a draw when that rule is enabled for the build.

## Controls

The exact button layout may be tuned during testing, but the landscape design uses:

- **Left side:** movement joystick or directional pad.
- **Right side:** attack, kick/heavy action, block, and jump/dodge buttons.
- **Top HUD:** both health bars and the match timer.

See `CONTROLS_AND_TUTORIAL.md` for the tutorial script and accessibility requirements.

## Rematch

After a result, choose **Rematch** to play the same mode again or **Back to Menu** to change mode or settings. Online rematches require both players to accept within the displayed time.

## Online 1v1

Online 1v1 is planned for separate devices. It is not a two-player same-phone mode. Matchmaking may show a connection check before the fight begins. If a player disconnects, follow the message on screen; do not close the app while a reconnect countdown is active.

## Fair play

Do not use modified clients, scripts, network manipulation, or exploits. Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against other players. Repeated abuse can result in a match cancellation, temporary restriction, or account action under the final Terms of Service.

## Ads and rewards

Ads, if enabled in your build, should appear only at natural breaks. A rewarded ad is always optional and its reward must be described before you accept it. Never install unofficial versions that promise unlimited currency or ad removal; those builds can steal account data and are not supported.

## Troubleshooting

- **Controls feel delayed:** check your touch screen, close background apps, and test a stable connection for online matches.
- **Connection warning:** move to a more stable network and wait for the reconnect message.
- **Missing reward:** capture the result screen and use the official support route; never send a password or token.
- **A player is abusive:** finish safely if possible, then use the report flow and block option.

## Safety reminder

Take regular breaks. Keep the screen at a comfortable brightness and play in a stable network environment. If the game is rated or age-gated differently in a particular region, the store listing and final legal documents take priority over this draft.
