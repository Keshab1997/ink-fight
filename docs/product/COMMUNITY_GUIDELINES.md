# Community Guidelines

INK FIGHT is a competitive game, but the community should remain safe and welcoming.

## Do

- Treat opponents, teammates, moderators, and support staff with respect.
- Use the report and block tools for behaviour that breaks these guidelines.
- Give useful bug reports without exposing personal information.
- Celebrate good plays and accept a loss without targeting the other player.
- Keep usernames and profile content suitable for the intended audience and store rating.

## Do not

- Use hate speech, slurs, threats, targeted harassment, or sexual content.
- Impersonate Keshab Studios, moderators, support staff, or another player.
- Share passwords, tokens, payment details, addresses, or private contact information.
- Encourage cheating, fraud, account theft, or evasion of moderation.
- Abuse the report system or intentionally create misleading evidence.

## Moderation

Reports may be reviewed with relevant match metadata. Moderation actions can include warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, or account action. The final process, appeal route, and retention period must be matched to the launched product and published privacy notice.

## Reporting safely

Use the in-game report flow or the official support address. Do not post personal data in a public issue. If there is an immediate real-world safety concern, contact local emergency services; game support is not an emergency service.
