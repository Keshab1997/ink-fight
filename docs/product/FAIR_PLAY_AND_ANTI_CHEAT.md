# Fair Play and Anti-Cheat Policy

## Our promise

INK FIGHT should reward timing, positioning, and practice—not modified clients, payment, or network abuse. The rules below apply to bot and online play, with stronger enforcement for competitive modes.

## Prohibited behaviour

- Modified, repackaged, or instrumented game clients used to gain an advantage.
- Scripts, macros, automation, memory editing, speed manipulation, or impossible input rates.
- Network tampering, packet injection, replaying invalid messages, or attempting to impersonate the server.
- Exploiting a bug after it has been publicly documented instead of reporting it.
- Account sharing, boosting, win trading, or intentionally manipulating matchmaking.
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual exploitation, or targeted abuse.
- Fraudulent purchases, chargeback abuse, or attempts to bypass ad/reward rules.

## Technical safeguards

The implementation should use server-authoritative match outcomes, signed or validated session data, input-rate checks, sequence numbers, server-side cooldowns, and anomaly logging that avoids collecting unnecessary personal data. Detection must support review and should not rely on a single noisy signal.

## Enforcement ladder

Depending on severity and evidence, action may include:

1. Warning or education.
2. Removal of an invalid result or reward.
3. Temporary matchmaking restriction.
4. Temporary suspension.
5. Permanent account restriction.

Severe threats, fraud, or attacks on the service may be escalated as permitted by law and the final Terms of Service.

## Bug reports

Report exploitable bugs through the official support route. Do not post working cheat instructions publicly. Include device, app version, match ID if available, steps to reproduce, and a short screen recording only when it does not expose private information.
