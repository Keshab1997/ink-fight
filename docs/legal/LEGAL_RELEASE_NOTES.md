# Legal and Store Release Notes

This checklist prevents the draft documents from being mistaken for final legal copy.

## Before any public beta

- [ ] Choose the legal publisher/entity name.
- [ ] Provide a monitored support and privacy email.
- [ ] Decide the target countries and age/content rating.
- [ ] Confirm the real authentication, analytics, crash, ads, hosting, and realtime providers.
- [ ] Map each data field and SDK to the Privacy Policy and Play Console Data Safety form.
- [ ] Choose account/data retention periods.
- [ ] Implement and test account deletion.
- [ ] Publish the final Privacy Policy and Terms at stable HTTPS URLs.
- [ ] Publish Community Guidelines, Fair Play rules, and support instructions.
- [ ] Confirm ad consent behaviour and non-personalised ad fallback.
- [ ] Have the final wording reviewed for the intended jurisdictions.

## Never commit

- Production tokens, API keys, private signing keys, keystores, `key.properties`, or provider secret files.
- Personal support inbox credentials.
- Raw crash dumps containing personal data.
- Private player reports.
