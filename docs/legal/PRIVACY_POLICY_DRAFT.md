## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how the publisher collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use INK FIGHT, its online services, support channels, and related websites. It is a draft for the planned product and must be updated if the final implementation differs.

## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features enabled in your build, we may process:

- **Account and profile information:** a server-generated player ID, display name, selected cosmetics, sign-in provider identifier, and account status.
- **Gameplay information:** match mode, match result, ruleset version, connection outcome, settings, and earned cosmetic entitlements.
- **Technical information:** app version, device model, operating system version, language/region settings, crash and performance diagnostics, and coarse network/latency information.
- **Advertising and consent information:** consent choices and advertising-related identifiers or signals processed by the advertising provider, subject to the choices available in your region.
- **Support and reports:** the information you choose to send when you contact support or report a player/bug. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment card numbers, or government IDs.

The final product should not collect more information than it needs. Do not enable a data field, SDK, analytics event, chat feature, or identifier without updating this policy and the applicable store disclosures.

## 3. How we use information

We may use information to:

- provide the game, matchmaking, saved progress, cosmetics, and support;
- secure sessions, prevent fraud and cheating, and enforce fair-play rules;
- diagnose crashes, performance problems, network failures, and reward errors;
- understand aggregate feature use and improve balance and reliability;
- deliver ads and optional rewards according to your consent choices and the advertising provider's rules;
- comply with law, platform requirements, and legitimate safety requests.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use gameplay information to make decisions unrelated to operating, securing, or improving the service without a lawful basis and appropriate notice.
