# Support and Reporting Playbook

## Official routes

- In-game **Report Player** flow for cheating, harassment, or match abuse.
- In-game **Report a Bug** flow for crashes, controls, display, or reward problems.
- Official support email: `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]`.
- Public repository issues: use only for non-sensitive development bugs; never post personal data or account credentials.

## What to include

- App version.
- Device model and operating system version.
- Mode and approximate time of the match.
- Match ID or result ID if shown.
- What happened and the steps that reproduce it.
- Screenshot or screen recording only when it does not reveal private information.

## What not to include

Never request or share:

- Passwords, authentication tokens, one-time codes, private keys, or keystores.
- Payment card numbers or government identity documents.
- Full advertising IDs unless the approved support process specifically requires a safe diagnostic identifier.
- Private information about another player.

## Response categories

1. Account access and data deletion.
2. Purchase or reward issue.
3. Crash or performance issue.
4. Online match or reconnect issue.
5. Cheating or harassment report.
6. Privacy or consent request.

Support should acknowledge receipt, avoid promising a result before review, and provide a safe escalation route. The final response-time target and retention period must be chosen before launch.

## Moderation review

Reports should be linked to a match or account reference when possible. Reviewers should see only the information needed to decide the case. Player reports must not become a public feed.
